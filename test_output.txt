2.1   Diversity entropy (DE)
Diversity   entropy   (DE)   can   calculate   the   complexity of an arbitrary   time   series in   a dynamic   manner [ 15 ]. Unlike   conventional   entropy   methods,   in   the   DE algorithm,   distribution   of   the   signal   states   has   been described   with   the   help   of   pattern   similarity.   Internal change   of   a   signal   pattern   can   be   better   re ected   by this algorithm.
